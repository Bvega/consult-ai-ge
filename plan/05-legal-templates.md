# Legal templates

These are plain-language starting drafts, not legal advice. Have a lawyer in your jurisdiction review them before taking payment. Items in `[[double brackets]]` must be filled in.

The site already carries short versions at `site/privacy.html` and `site/terms.html`. Keep those in step with whatever your lawyer approves.

## Refund policy

> If the course is not right for you, email us within 14 days of your cohort's start date (or within 14 days of purchase for the self-paced course) and we will refund you in full. You do not need to give a reason.

## Earnings disclaimer

Use on the sales page and in the welcome email.

> Consult AI-ge teaches a method for turning professional experience into an advisory offer. We do not promise that you will find clients or earn any particular amount. Results depend on your field, your effort and your network.

## Professional-practice disclaimer

Use in Module 0 and Module 6.

> This course teaches you to offer advice based on your experience. It does not qualify or license you to practise law, medicine, accounting, financial advice, engineering or any other regulated profession. If your field is regulated, check with your licensing body what you may offer once retired or unlicensed, and describe your service accordingly.

## Confidentiality reminder for learners

Use in Module 0 and Module 1.

> Your stories belong to you; your former employer's confidential information does not. Before you use a story, remove names, figures and details that could identify an organisation, client or patient. If you signed a confidentiality or non-compete agreement, reread it. Never paste confidential documents into an AI tool.

## Testimonial consent

Send at the end of the pilot.

> May we quote your feedback on our website and in our emails?
> ☐ Yes, with my full name and former role
> ☐ Yes, with my first name and field only
> ☐ No, please keep it private

## Recording notice for live calls

Say at the start of every call and put in the calendar invitation.

> This call is recorded so that members who cannot attend can watch later. Recordings are shared only with enrolled members. If you would rather not appear, keep your camera off and use the chat.

## Checklist before taking the first payment

- [ ] Business name and legal form decided: `[[sole proprietor / company]]`
- [ ] Privacy page and terms page filled in and published
- [ ] Refund policy visible on the sales page
- [ ] Earnings disclaimer visible on the sales page
- [ ] Sales tax or VAT position checked for where you and your buyers live
- [ ] A separate bank account or clear bookkeeping for course income
