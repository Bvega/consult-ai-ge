# Pricing and simple financial model

All figures are planning assumptions in US dollars. None of them come from market research; the pilot exists to test them.

## Prices

| Offer | Price | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| Founding Cohort | $297 | All modules, six live calls, worksheets, prompt library. In exchange: feedback each week and a testimonial if they are happy. |
| Self-paced | $397 | All modules, call recordings, worksheets, prompt library. |
| Course + cohort | $697 | Everything in self-paced plus six live calls and feedback on the learner's one-pager. |

Refund policy: full refund within 14 days of the start date, no questions asked. A generous refund matters more than a low price for a cautious audience.

## Pilot arithmetic

| Seats sold | Revenue at $297 |
|---|---|
| 6 (minimum to run) | $1,782 |
| 9 | $2,673 |
| 12 (full) | $3,564 |

Run the pilot if at least 6 people pay. Below that, refund, talk to everyone who showed interest, and revise the offer before trying again.

## Costs to expect

Look up current prices before committing; they change often.

| Item | Needed for | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Domain name | Launch | Annual fee |
| Website hosting | Launch | The site in `site/` fits free static-hosting tiers |
| Video calls | Pilot | Any tool that records and allows 60-minute group calls |
| Payment processing | Launch | A percentage plus a fixed fee per sale |
| Email tool | Launch | Free tiers usually cover a first list |
| Course platform | Self-paced version | Not needed for the pilot; see `plan/04-tech-stack.md` |
| Your time | Always | Roughly 4–6 hours a week during a cohort: one call, prep, and replies |

## How to test the price

1. Sell the pilot at $297. If all 12 seats go within a week of opening, the next price is too low.
2. In the week-6 feedback form, ask: "What would you have paid for this, knowing what you know now?"
3. Raise the price for each cohort until roughly one in three seriously interested people say no on price alone.

## Measures worth tracking

| Measure | Target for the pilot |
|---|---|
| Waitlist sign-ups before opening | 60 or more |
| Waitlist to paid | 10–20% |
| Learners who finish module 6 | 8 of 12 |
| Learners who publish an offer page | 8 of 12 |
| Learners who hold one paid call within 60 days | 4 of 12 |
| Would recommend (asked in week 6) | 10 of 12 |

The fifth row is the one that proves the course works. Follow up at 30 and 60 days to collect it.
