# Consult AI-ge — Project Brief

## Concept
A standalone course/product teaching retired professionals how to use AI tools to translate decades of career experience into consulting offerings — frameworks, checklists, and content — that younger, tech-fluent professionals can find, trust, and pay for.

## The Core Idea
Retired professionals have judgment, pattern-recognition, and hard-won experience but often lack:
- The tech fluency to package or market that knowledge
- A clear path back into paid work that doesn't mean "starting a second career from scratch"

Younger professionals have tool fluency and speed but lack:
- The seasoning that comes from having seen things go wrong before
- Access to mentors who understand both the problem *and* how to talk about it

Consult AI-ge sits in the middle: AI as the translator that turns a retiree's tacit knowledge into something visible, structured, and sellable — without requiring them to become a programmer or marketer first.

## Target Learner
Retired or semi-retired professionals (finance, healthcare admin, operations, law, engineering, etc.) who:
- Have deep domain experience
- Are open to consulting or advisory work post-retirement
- Are not tech-native but are willing to learn practical AI tools
- Want a low-friction way back into paid, flexible work

## What the Course Teaches (Not Just "How AI Works")
1. **Knowledge extraction** — using AI as a conversational partner to pull specific stories/situations out of memory and turn them into reusable frameworks
2. **Packaging** — turning a framework into a checklist, one-pager, slide deck, or simple website using AI tools, no coding or design skills required
3. **Positioning** — how to describe the offer so it's findable and credible to a younger audience (not "hire a retiree," but "get the judgment layer you don't have yet")
4. **Working across generations** — how to pair with tech-fluent junior professionals so both sides get value (their speed + your judgment)
5. **Light business basics** — pricing a first offer, running a single consulting call, staying inside "advisory," not building a whole agency

## Illustrative Use Case
Linda, a retired hospital finance director, talks through a real budget-audit crisis with an AI tool, which helps her turn it into a "3 warning signs" checklist. She builds a one-pager and simple site with AI, no design help needed. Marcus, a 28-year-old hospital analyst strong on dashboards but new to audits, finds her checklist, recognizes the exact problem he's nervous about, and books a call. AI didn't replace either of them — it gave Linda an easy way to make her experience visible to someone who needed it.

## Working Name
**Consult AI-ge**
(Playful pronunciation: "consult-age" — signals both "consulting" and "age/experience," with the AI tie-in front and center.)

## Format (open question)
- Standalone self-paced course
- Cohort-based with live sessions
- Hybrid: self-paced modules + optional community/cohort calls

## Origin
Idea emerged out of reflecting on Bolivar's own AI course experience and thinking about who else could benefit from a similarly practical, hands-on approach — then pivoted into its own separate business concept.
