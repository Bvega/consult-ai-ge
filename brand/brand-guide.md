# Brand guide

## Name

**Consult AI-ge**, said "consult-age". Consulting, plus age and experience, with AI in the middle.

- Always written with a capital C, capital AI, a hyphen and lower-case "ge".
- The first time it appears anywhere new, add the pronunciation: *Consult AI-ge (said "consult-age")*.

## Tagline

**Your experience, made visible.**

Alternates for specific uses:

- *The judgment layer, for hire.* (when speaking to younger professionals)
- *Thirty years of know-how. Six weeks to put it to work.* (advertising)

## Logo

`site/assets/logo.svg`: three concentric rings (ripples, or the rings of a tree) beside the name in a serif face, with "AI" in the accent colour. `site/assets/favicon.svg` is the ring mark alone.

Ink on Paper and white on Terracotta both exceed the 4.5:1 contrast ratio recommended for body text.

## Type

- **Headings:** a book serif. On the web: Iowan Old Style, Palatino, Georgia, in that order. In Canva or documents: Georgia or any similar serif.
- **Body:** the reader's own system sans-serif. In documents: Arial or Helvetica.
- **Size:** body text never smaller than 18px on screen or 12pt in print. Buttons at least 56px tall.

## Voice

We sound like a respected colleague who is glad to see you: warm, plain and exact.

| We are | We are not |
|---|---|
| Plain: short words, short sentences | Jargon-filled or breathless about technology |
| Respectful: the reader is the expert | Patronising about age or about computers |
| Specific: numbers, examples, Linda | Vague: "unlock your potential" |
| Honest about limits | Promising income or clients |

### Words

| Use | Avoid |
|---|---|
| experience, judgment, know-how | wisdom, legacy, golden years |
| retired professional | senior, elderly, boomer |
| advisory work, a paid call | side hustle, gig, encore career |
| AI assistant | bot, chatbot, "the AI" as if it were a person |
| younger professionals, people early in their careers | kids, millennials, Gen Z |
| type, ask, tell | prompt-engineer, leverage, harness |

### Three rules

1. Lead with the reader's experience, not with the technology.
2. Every claim about results carries the limit: we teach a method; we do not promise clients.
3. Linda and Marcus are always labelled as an illustration.

## Photography

Real people in ordinary rooms with natural light. Two people looking at the same screen is the picture of the brand. No stock images of robots, glowing brains or silver-haired couples on beaches.
