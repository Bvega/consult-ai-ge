# Facilitator guide

For whoever runs the six weekly live calls.

## Before the cohort

- [ ] Fix one day and time for all six calls and put all six in learners' calendars at once
- [ ] Test recording and screen sharing on your video tool
- [ ] Send the welcome email with Module 0 one week before the first call (`marketing/email-sequences.md`, sequence C)
- [ ] Offer a 15-minute one-to-one "tech check" to anyone who wants it before week 1. Expect about a third to take it. It prevents most early drop-outs.

## The weekly rhythm

| Day | What you do |
|---|---|
| Monday | Email the week's module and worksheet |
| Thursday | Short reminder: the "Bring to the call" item and the joining link |
| Call day | Run the call |
| Day after | Email the recording and one highlight from the group |

## Shape of every call (60 minutes)

| Minutes | Segment | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5 | Welcome | Read the recording notice. One-line check-in from each person. |
| 5–25 | Show and tell | Everyone shares their "Bring to the call" item. Keep each to two minutes. |
| 25–45 | Hot seat | Two volunteers share a screen and get help with whatever is stuck. |
| 45–55 | Next week | Preview the next module. Demonstrate its first prompt live. |
| 55–60 | Commitments | Each person says the one thing they will finish by next week. |

Rules of the room, stated in week 1:

1. Nothing confidential, about former employers or about each other.
2. No such thing as a silly question about technology.
3. Feedback names what worked first, then one thing to change.

## Week-by-week notes

### Call 1, after Module 1: stories

- **Aim:** everyone leaves believing their stories are valuable.
- **Show and tell:** strongest story title and its signal.
- **Listen for:** stories with no signal ("we worked hard and it turned out well"). Ask: "What did you notice before anyone else did?"
- **Common snag:** worry about confidentiality. Repeat the rule: disguise it or drop it.

### Call 2, after Module 2: frameworks

- **Aim:** every framework is specific and earned.
- **Show and tell:** read the framework aloud. Group says which item landed hardest.
- **Listen for:** generic items that an AI tool supplied. Ask: "What's the story behind that one?" If there is none, it goes.
- **Common snag:** "it's too obvious". Have a younger guest or another member confirm it is not obvious to them.

### Call 3, after Module 3: packaging

- **Aim:** everyone has a live web address.
- **Show and tell:** open each page for ten seconds, then say what you understood.
- **Hot seat:** this is the most technical week. Allow three hot seats and shorten show and tell.
- **Common snag:** perfectionism. "Published and plain beats perfect and private."

### Call 4, after Module 4: positioning

- **Aim:** every one-sentence offer is understood by the group.
- **Show and tell:** each person reads their sentence; one other person says back what they think is on offer.
- **Listen for:** buzzwords, and offers that are too broad. Ask: "Who exactly, and what are they worried about this month?"
- **Common snag:** discomfort with self-promotion. Reframe it as handing out something useful.

### Call 5, after Module 5: generations

- **Aim:** learners hear how younger professionals actually responded.
- **Show and tell:** the most surprising thing a younger person said.
- **Consider:** inviting one or two early-career guests for the first half to answer questions about how they look for help.
- **Common snag:** not enough conversations. Pair members to swap introductions.

### Call 6, after Module 6: first paid call

- **Aim:** every price is said aloud and every booking link works.
- **Show and tell:** each person says "The session is [price]" and shares the link. The group books nothing, but clicks through to check that it works.
- **Close:** each person states what they will do in the next 30 days. Announce the 30-day and 60-day follow-up.
- **After the call:** send the feedback form and the testimonial consent question (`plan/05-legal-templates.md`).

## Supporting people who fall behind

- A missed week is normal. Email them the single most important step from that module, not the whole thing.
- If someone misses two calls, telephone them. A voice works better than another email for this audience.
- Modules 1 and 2 cannot be skipped. Modules 3 and 4 can be done in either order.

## Feedback form (week 6)

1. What did you produce during the course? (story bank / framework / one-pager / web page / offer / booking link)
2. Which module was most useful? Which was least?
3. Where did you get stuck?
4. Was the pace too fast, about right, or too slow?
5. Knowing what you know now, what would you have paid for this?
6. Would you recommend it to a former colleague? Why, or why not?
7. May we quote you? (see consent options)

## Follow-up after the cohort

| When | What |
|---|---|
| 30 days | Email: "Have you held a paid call yet? What is in the way?" |
| 60 days | Same question, plus an invitation to a free alumni call |

Record the answers. The proportion who hold a paid call within 60 days is the number that proves the course works.
