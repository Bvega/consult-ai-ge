# Consult AI-ge: the course

Six weeks. One outcome: a published advisory offer built from your own experience, and a plan for your first paid call.

## Who this is for

You have spent decades in a field such as finance, healthcare administration, operations, law or engineering. You are retired or close to it. You would like some paid, flexible advisory work, and you do not want to start a second career to get it. You are not a "tech person" and you do not need to become one.

## What you will have at the end

1. A **story bank**: five real situations from your career, written down
2. A **framework**: one of those stories turned into a named, reusable tool
3. A **one-pager and a simple web page** that show that tool to the world
4. A **one-sentence offer** that a 28-year-old in your field understands immediately
5. **Three conversations** with younger professionals who could use your judgment
6. A **price, a booking link and a call plan** for your first paid session

Each module has the same shape:

- **The idea**: a short read
- **Watch it happen**: the same example, Linda, followed through every module
- **Your turn**: the exercise, with the exact words to type into your AI assistant
- **Done when**: how to know you have finished
- **Bring to the call**: what to share at the weekly live session

## Three promises

1. **No jargon without a translation.** If a technical word appears, it is explained the first time.
2. **No coding and no design skill needed.** If you can write an email, you can do every exercise.
3. **AI is the assistant, never the expert.** You bring the judgment. The tool helps you get it out of your head and onto a page.
