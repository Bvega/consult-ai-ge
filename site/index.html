How it works

Linda's story, in four steps

Linda is the example we follow through the whole course.

  1. She talks; the AI asks questions

    Linda, a retired hospital finance director, tells an AI assistant about a budget audit that nearly went badly wrong. It keeps asking "was there an earlier sign?" until she remembers three.

  2. The story becomes a checklist

    Together they turn it into "3 Warning Signs Your Department Budget Is Hiding a Problem". Linda throws out everything generic and keeps only what she saw herself.

  3. She publishes it in an afternoon

    Using a ready-made template, she makes a one-page PDF and a simple web page. No designer and no code.

  4. Marcus finds it and books a call

    Marcus, 28, is a hospital analyst who is brilliant with dashboards and nervous about his first audit. He recognises his own problem in her checklist and books an hour with her.

Linda and Marcus are an illustration, not real customers. AI did not replace either of them. It gave Linda an easy way to make her experience visible to someone who needed it.

Questions

What people ask before joining

I am not good with technology. Can I really do this?

Yes. If you can write an email, you can do every exercise. You type in plain English and the AI assistant replies in plain English. The first module is a gentle setup, and anyone who wants one gets a one-to-one tech check before week 1.

What do I need to buy?

Nothing to begin with. The free versions of the tools we use are enough to complete the course. You need a computer or tablet and an internet connection.

How much time does it take?

Two to three hours a week for six weeks, plus the one-hour live call. Week 3, when you build your page, takes a little longer.

Will the AI write my expertise for me?

No, and you would not want it to. The AI asks questions and tidies up your answers. The knowledge is yours. A good part of the course is learning to spot and remove the generic text these tools like to add.

Is my former employer's information safe?

You will never be asked to share anything confidential. The first rule of the course is to change names and figures before you type, and to leave out any story covered by an agreement you signed.

My profession is licensed. Can I still advise?

Often yes, but the limits differ by profession and place. The course teaches you to share experience and judgment, not to practise a regulated profession, and it tells you what to check with your licensing body first.

Will you find me clients?

No. We teach you how to become findable and how to hold your first conversations, and you do the reaching out. We do not promise clients or income.

What if it is not for me?

Tell us within 14 days of the start date and we will refund you in full.
