The Story Starter

Five questions that turn one career story into the start of a checklist. Twenty minutes, with a pen or with an AI assistant.

Before you begin

Think of a time at work when something nearly went wrong, or did. Choose one you remember well. Change the names and leave out anything confidential.

The five questions

  1. What was at stake? Where were you, and what would have happened if it had gone badly?
  2. What did you notice first? Not the crisis itself. The small thing before it.
  3. Looking back, was there an even earlier sign? Something you dismissed at the time.
  4. What did you do, and what did you decide not to do?
  5. What would you tell someone facing this tomorrow? One sentence.

Now look at questions 2 and 3

Those two answers are warning signs that a person with five years' experience would probably miss. Write each as a plain sentence beginning "Watch for…". You have the first two lines of a checklist that only you could have written.

Prefer to talk it through?

Open any AI assistant (Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini) and type this:

I want to capture a story from my career. Ask me these five questions one at a time and wait for my answer each time: what was at stake; what I noticed first; whether there was an even earlier sign; what I did and chose not to do; and what I would tell someone facing this tomorrow. Then write up my answers in under 200 words, using my own words.
